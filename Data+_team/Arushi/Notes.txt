RNA Sequencing 

Start with the samples (18 in our case) 
Isolate RNAs 
Then generate cDNA and fragment them, add linkers 
Sequencing is done in a massive platform (ILLUMINA) 
This creates short reads and sequenced in a pair 
Produce millions of the sequenced fragments which then get fed into many downstream analysis 
 

Challenges 

Purity and quality, quantity can be an issue but not so much so with RNA sequencing 
RNA is fragile compared to DNA 
 

Metric/ Interpretation 

RIN = RNA integrity number 
0 bad to 10 (good) 
Runs for about 5 minutes 
 

 

Stranded vs Unstranded Data 

How is the strand information encoded in your data 
Data is stranded and it's on the positive/negative strand (output after the run) 
 

Bulk vs Single cell RNA seq 

Interested in cell type and what each of them are doing and behaving differently so choose a single cell in this case. Reveals heterogeneity 
Single cell data is very sparse, massive matrix of the genes and very spread 
Try both when you can since they both are complimentary 
Low heterogeneity don't just do single cell 
 

02: Alignment 

Central to most genomic research

Introns are copied into RNA and then cut out and discarded leaving only exon 
There can be spaces in the alignment 
 

RNA Mapping Strategies
